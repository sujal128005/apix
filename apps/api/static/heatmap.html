Line thickness follows the size of the movement; colour follows its direction. Corridors are drawn as arcs so both directions of a city pair stay distinguishable. Thresholds are shown in the legend rather than left implicit — a colour scale a reader cannot see invites them to infer a severity that was never claimed.
The outline follows the official Government of India depiction, with Jammu & Kashmir and Ladakh shown in full as Indian territory. It is a schematic locator, not a survey product: traced at around 130 points, enough to orient a reader and not offered as a cartographic authority. If the ministry licenses a Survey of India basemap it replaces this outline and nothing else on the page changes.
